Philosophy

The Art of the Cut.

At Theylina Barber, we believe that grooming is more than a routine — it is a ritual. Situated in the heart of London, our space is designed for gentlemen who appreciate the finer things in life and understand that true luxury lies in the details.

Our master barbers are craftsmen of the highest order, bringing decades of combined experience to every chair. From the perfect hot towel straight razor shave to a precision tailored cut, every service is executed with unhurried perfection.

Step into our private members' club atmosphere, leave the noise of the city behind, and let us take care of the rest.
